WHAT SHOULD WE DOODLE?

HOW TO PLAY:

1. Tap the “What Should We Doodle?” button to shuffle up a silly prompt.

2. Draw your interpretation of the prompt together or separately.

See below for different ways to play!

Different Ways to Doodle Together:

Everyone Draws It

Tap to get a silly prompt, then everyone draws their own version. Take as much time as you need, or set a 2-minute timer if you want a challenge. Reveal at the same time and enjoy how differently everyone pictured it!

Pass the Doodle

One person starts drawing. After 30 seconds, they pass it to the next person, who keeps adding details for 30 more seconds. Keep going until the drawing becomes wonderfully weird and complete!

Guess the Doodle

One person secretly looks at the prompt and draws while everyone else guesses what it is. The drawer cannot give clues by talking, but they can nod or shake their head!

One Line Each

Pick a prompt, share the paper, and take turns adding only one line, shape, or detail at a time, working together to complete the picture.

Caption That

Each person secretly gets a different prompt and draws it. When everyone is done, pass your paper to the next person, who writes a funny caption underneath the drawing based on what they think is happening.

Fold the paper back so only the caption is showing, then pass again. The next person draws a brand-new picture underneath based only on that caption.

Keep alternating captions and drawings until the paper is full, then unfold everything at the end to reveal the wonderfully ridiculous evolution!

If you only have two players, just secretly draw your prompt, swap papers, and caption each other’s drawings.

This game works best with kids who can read and write independently since the prompts stay secret.
